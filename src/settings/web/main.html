      Dexmenu

      Your own right-click menu for Windows, built from layouts you arrange yourself.

      Made out of frustration with Windows' right-click menu: little control over what is in it, and a long list of items, some of them hardly useful. With Dexmenu you can create your own menu, with your functionalities, and the theme you want.
